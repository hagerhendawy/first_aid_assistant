TITLE: Unresponsive Person and CPR
SOURCE: Compiled from public guidance of the American Heart Association, European Resuscitation Council and British Red Cross
---
Check if the person responds by tapping their shoulders and asking loudly if they are all right. If there is no response, shout for help and call emergency services.

Open the airway by tilting the head back and lifting the chin. Look, listen and feel for normal breathing for no more than ten seconds. If the person is breathing normally, place them in the recovery position and monitor them until help arrives.

If the person is not breathing normally, start CPR:
Place the heel of your hand in the centre of the chest, other hand on top.
Push hard and fast, about 5 to 6 centimetres deep at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute.
After 30 compressions give 2 rescue breaths if you are trained. If you are not trained, continue compressions only.
Send someone to find an AED (automated external defibrillator). Switch it on and follow the voice instructions.
Do not stop until emergency services take over, the person starts breathing, or you are too exhausted to continue.
